title: How the score works
description: Why every listing carries a 0–100 score, a letter grade and a maintenance status, and why the model is published as data.
---

A plugin you install runs with your permissions, so before installing you want to know whether other people use it, whether someone still maintains it, and whether it shows its credentials. The score turns those three questions into three dimensions — popularity, maintenance and quality — blended into one 0–100 number, a letter grade and a maintenance status.
